## Hi, I'm Karim 

Statistician and econometrician based in Toronto, with more than a decade of experience working with justice and social data and teaching statistics.

### 🎙️ The Residual
A podcast where I take one published finding on inequality (gender, race and class), rerun it from the authors' own data and code, and report what holds up, what doesn't, and what changes when you push on it.

Every episode's code lives in **[the-residual](https://github.com/karimsouidi/the-residual)**, and every number can be reproduced in a few minutes.

|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hiring discrimination, 2001 vs 2019–21 | Bertrand & Mullainathan (2004); Kline, Rose & Walters (2022) |
